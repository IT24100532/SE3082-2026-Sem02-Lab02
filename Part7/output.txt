Part 7: make
main.c calls add() from utils.c (declared in utils.h). The Makefile is the one from the lab sheet.

$ make
gcc -Wall -c main.c
gcc -Wall -c utils.c
gcc -Wall -o myapp main.o utils.o
$ ./myapp
2 + 3 = 5

$ make clean
rm -f *.o myapp

$ make -j4          (builds main.o and utils.o at the same time)
gcc -Wall -c main.c
gcc -Wall -c utils.c
gcc -Wall -o myapp main.o utils.o
